How to use FTCIntel

Getting Started

Sign UpRegister your team number and email — you'll receive an access token by email.
LoginEnter your team number and token to access the dashboard.
Guest ModeBrowse intel and data without connecting. Click CONNECT to enable scouting.

Scouting

ConnectClick CONNECT on the guest banner, enter your Scout ID and select your event.
Scout TabEnter target team, match number, alliance position, and scoring counters, then hit SAVE MATCH.
Scout IDsScout IDs are managed by your team admin in the Admin Panel.

Intel & Data

Intel TabSearch any team to see average teleop score, auto performance by position, and match history chart.
Event FilterFilter intel by current event or across all events.
Data TabView all raw scouting records submitted by your team.

Events

Switch EventUse the Events tab to switch your active event — intel filtering will update accordingly.
Add EventNew events can be added by any logged-in user via the Connect modal or by admins in the Admin Panel.

Admin Panel

AccessClick Admin Panel in the sidebar and enter the admin password received at signup.
Manage ScoutsAdd or remove Scout IDs that are authorized to submit match data.
Edit DataAdmins can edit or delete individual scouting records from the database.
